Questions

Good to know

Which one is for me?

If you are getting a sales or service team ready for real customer calls, pick Sales Coach. If you are getting yourself, or a group of candidates, ready for a job interview, pick Interview Coach.

Am I talking to a real person?

No. The buyer, the hiring manager and the coach are AI voices that sound like real people. That is what makes the practice feel like the real call.

Do I need an app or a webcam?

No. You call one phone number from your own phone and say your access code.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. Canceling stops the next charge, and your plan stays active through the period you already paid for.
