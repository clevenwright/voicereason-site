Which AI practice tool works by phone for staff without laptops, and what does it cost for 30 people?

VoiceReason works on a regular phone call. Your team member dials one number from their own phone, says or enters their six-digit access code, and practices a real conversation with an AI customer: an incredibly lifelike AI voice that sounds human. There is no laptop, no app, no download and no login for them. For a team of 30, the Practice plan is $2,070 a month.

Built for teams who are not at a desk

A lot of customer-facing work does not happen at a desk: technicians between jobs, home care staff, front desk teams, people in retail, field service and the trades. They all carry a phone.

  • One number, one code. Each person gets a six-digit access code. They call, say or enter the code, and the practice call starts.
  • Any time, anywhere they can talk. From the truck between appointments, on a break, or at home the night before a tough visit.
  • Nothing to install or remember. No app store, no password, no training on a new tool.
  • A report by email after every call. What went well and what to try next time, measured against your own playbook if you add one.

How it works

  1. Describe the customer. In a sentence or a page, say who your team talks to and what they push back on. Or start from one of ours.
  2. Add your playbook if you have one. Every report then measures the call against the way your team sells.
  3. Your team member dials one number from their own phone and says or enters their six-digit access code. No app, no download, no login.
  4. They talk to the customer. An AI voice that sounds like a real person stays in character and pushes back like a real customer would.
  5. They get a written report by email after every call. On Practice + Coach, an incredibly lifelike AI coach also comes on the same call right after the practice call ends and talks it through with them.

What it costs for a team of 30

Every price is on our pricing page. Here is the math for 30 people who make practice calls. Your own manager login does not use a seat.

8,400 practice minutes a month for the team, up to 45 minutes on one call, and a written report after every call.

Practice + Coach, 30 seats

$3,870 a month (30 x $129).

What a seat is

One seat is one person who makes practice calls, with one access code. If 30 people practice, you need 30 seats. Managers see every call and report on the Calls page without using a seat.
