First, on the phone

A real interview, on your phone.

You call one number from your own phone. A hiring manager for the role in your posting picks up. It has read your resume and the job, asks about your own work, and runs the interview the way a real one would.

  • Leads the call: introduces themselves, sets the agenda and tells you how the interview will go.
  • Asks behavioral questions for the role: tell me about a time you did what this job needs.
  • Asks about your own experience, by name, from your resume.
  • Digs into your answers. A vague story gets a "walk me through your part in that."
  • Handles each gap the way a real hiring manager would. It digs into experience in the same field instead of just asking "do you have a degree," and it asks plainly about any license the job needs.
  • Interviews run up to 45 minutes on Practice and 60 minutes on Practice + Coach, as long as a real one might.
Example

Written for this page to show how it goes · not a recorded call · role: operations coordinator

Interviewer

Thanks for making the time. I lead operations here, and this role reports to me. I will ask about a few things you have done, then leave time for your questions. Sound good?

You

Sounds good.

Interviewer

Your resume says you ran scheduling for a team of twelve at the warehouse. Tell me about a time a schedule fell apart and you had to fix it fast.

You

We had a big order come in and we got everyone reassigned and it worked out.

Interviewer

Walk me through your part in that. What did you do first?

Interviewer, later

This role uses a lot of data reporting, and I do not see that on your resume yet. How have you used numbers to make a decision at work?

Then, the same phone call

Guidance built from your resume, the job and every answer you just gave.

On Practice + Coach, the AI coach comes on the same call right after the interviewer hangs up. No app to open, no link to click. Your AI coach works from your resume and the job posting, and from what you actually said.

  • Does not open with a grade. It asks how you think it went, and gives you room to spot things yourself.
  • Shows where an answer missed what the job is asking for
  • Points you to a stronger story from your own resume when you had a better one to tell
  • When a gap comes up, like a degree the job prefers, shows how to speak to it honestly
  • Rewrites your answer using a story you told. If a number is missing, it leaves a blank for you to fill in instead of guessing.
Example

Written for this page to show how it goes · not a recorded call · same example as above

Coach

That was a solid interview. Before I share anything, how did the schedule question feel to you?

You

Honestly, I rushed it. I knew the story but it came out flat.

Coach

Good catch. You said "we got everyone reassigned." The interviewer wanted your part. Your resume says you built the shift swap sheet. That is your story. Try it again with what you did first, what you did next, and how it ended.

Coach, on the gap

On data reporting, you do not need to pretend. Say what you have done, like tracking overtime by week, then say how you would grow into the rest. If you know the number, add it. If not, leave it blank until you do.

Questions

Good to know

Is the interviewer a real person?

No. The interviewer and the coach are AI, built to feel like the real interview so the practice carries over.

What kind of questions does the interviewer ask?

A structured, behavioral interview for the role in your job posting. Questions like "tell me about a time you..." built from the job and your resume, questions about the gaps, and follow-ups on what you actually say.

Do I need an app or a webcam?

No. You call one phone number from your own phone and say your six-digit access code. No app, no webcam, no login on the call.

How long is a practice interview?

Up to 45 minutes on Practice and up to 60 minutes on Practice + Coach.

What do the plans cost?

Practice is $69 a month with 280 practice minutes. Practice + Coach is $129 a month with 420 practice minutes and a live AI coach on the same call. A smaller Starter plan is coming soon.

Do I get feedback on Practice?

Yes. Every plan gets a written report after every call, with the full transcript. Practice + Coach adds the live AI coach on the same call.

Can I practice for more than one job?

Yes. You can rebuild your interview with a new job posting or an updated resume. Your setup page shows how many rebuilds you have left.

Is a plan for one person?

Yes. Each purchase is one seat for one person, with their own resume and their own target job.

Can I buy for a group of candidates?

Group plans are coming soon, where each person gets their own setup link. Until then, each person can buy their own plan.

What happens to my resume?

Your resume and the job posting are used to build your practice interview and your feedback. Pyrigen and the AI and technology providers that run VoiceReason process them for that. Who can use Interview Coach?

Adults 18 and over. Each plan is for one person preparing for their own interviews.

Is this a tool for getting answers during a real interview?

No. VoiceReason is for practice only. You build the skill before the interview and walk in ready on your own.
