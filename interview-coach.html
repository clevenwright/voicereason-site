The method

Built on the behavioral interview.

Your interviewer runs a structured, behavioral interview. It asks about real things you have done, then follows up on how you did them. It is the style hiring research backs.

The interview

An interviewer you would swear was real.

  • The interviewer leads the call: introduces themselves, sets the agenda and follows up on what you say.
  • It digs into your answers. A vague story gets a "walk me through your part in that."
  • Interviews run up to 45 minutes on Practice and 60 minutes on Practice + Coach, as long as a real one might.

Then, the coaching

Coaching built from your resume, the job and every answer you just gave.

Your coach works from your resume and the job posting. It shows where your answers missed what the job asks for, and how to answer better using your own experience, without making anything up.

  • Shows where an answer missed what the job is asking for
  • When a gap comes up, like a degree the job prefers, shows how to speak to it honestly
  • Rewrites your answer using a story you told, or points you to one on your resume. If a number is missing, it leaves a blank for you to fill in.
  • On Practice + Coach, the coach comes on the same call when the interviewer hangs up and talks it through with you

Every plan gets this coaching in a written report after every call, with the full transcript.

Questions

Good to know

Is the interviewer a real person?

No. The interviewer and the coach are AI voices that sound like real people. That is what makes it feel like the real interview.

What kind of questions does the interviewer ask?

A structured, behavioral interview for the role in your job posting. Questions like "tell me about a time you..." built from the job and your resume, plus follow-ups on what you actually say.

Is this a tool for getting answers during a real interview?

No. VoiceReason is for practice only. Nothing hidden. You build the skill before the interview and walk in ready on your own.

Do I need an app or a webcam?

No. You call one phone number from your own phone and say your access code.
