Recording and consent

What we keep, and what we never record.

Plain English first, because this is the question people actually have. Last updated 26 August 2026.

The short version

VoiceReason records practice calls only. A practice call is a conversation between one of your people and an AI buyer that we operate. There is no customer on the line and no third party on the line. We do not record real sales appointments, and the product has no ability to do so.

Every practice call opens with a spoken notice before anything else is said. Word for word, it is:

Thanks for calling VoiceReason.AI. This call is being recorded. Can I have your access code?

That notice is the first thing said on every line we operate, free or paid, with no exceptions. Continuing the call after that notice is how consent is given. If someone does not want the call kept, they hang up and nothing is kept.

The notice says recorded because that is exactly what happens. The conversation is captured and kept, as audio and as text.

What gets kept

For each practice call we keep a written transcript, the coaching report generated from it, and basic call details such as the time, length and the access code used.

The audio exists, and you cannot play it back yet. The service that runs the call retains the audio of it along with the transcript. What we surface to you today is the transcript and the coaching report. Playback inside your account is on our roadmap and is not built yet, so if you need the audio of a specific call before then, ask us and we will get it for you.

Why we keep it

The coaching report is produced from the transcript. Every claim in a report is checked against a specific line in the transcript before the report is sent, which is only possible if the transcript exists. The account owner can also read the transcript to see the coaching in context.

Who can see it

The person who made the call receives their own report by email. The owner of the account the access code belongs to can see the calls, transcripts and reports made under that account. Nobody outside the account can see them.

We do not sell anything we hold from a call, we do not share it with other customers, and we do not use your practice conversations to train models for anyone else.

Deleting it

You can ask us to delete what we hold for a call at any time, meaning the transcript, the coaching report and the audio, for a single call, for one person, or for a whole account. Email privacy@voicereason.ai from the address on the account and we will action it within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

Closing an account triggers deletion of everything we hold for it.

If you called our free line and did not create an account, email privacy@voicereason.ai with the phone number you called from and the approximate time, and we will find the call and delete it.

State recording laws

Some states require every person on a call to consent to being recorded. The spoken notice at the start of every call is how we obtain that consent, and it is given before any substantive conversation happens.

Because the only people ever on a VoiceReason call are your own person and our AI buyer, no customer, client or member of the public is ever recorded by this product. If you use a separate tool to record real appointments, that is between you and that tool, and the laws that apply to it are not covered by this page.

This page describes how the product works. It is not legal advice about your own obligations.
