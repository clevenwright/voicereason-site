Guide · Inside a practice call

What happens on a VoiceReason.AI practice interview call, start to finish?

It helps to know what to expect, so you can spend your energy on your answers instead of wondering what comes next. Here is the whole thing, step by step, from adding your resume to reading your report.

VoiceReason.AI Interview Coach: every plan includes the live AI coach on the same call. See the plans.

Before the call: add your resume and the job posting

Pick a plan, add your resume and paste the job posting. Your interviewer is built from both. It is a hiring manager for the role in your posting, with your resume in front of them.

A quick tip before you upload: take out anything you would rather we not store, such as a Social Security number, your date of birth or health details. The full details are in our Privacy Notice.

Step 1: Call the number from your own phone

Dial one number and say your six-digit access code. There is no app, no webcam and no login on the call. Find a quiet spot where you can talk out loud, the same way you would for a real phone interview.

Step 2: The interviewer picks up and leads

The interviewer runs the call the way a real one would. It introduces itself, sets the agenda and tells you how the interview will go. It speaks in an incredibly lifelike AI voice that sounds human, so the practice feels close to the real thing. You do not have to steer. Just listen and answer, like you would tomorrow.

Step 3: Behavioral questions for the role

Next come behavioral questions, the "tell me about a time" kind. They are built from the job in your posting, so you hear questions like "tell me about a time you did what this job needs." The interviewer also asks about your own experience, by name, from your resume. You will not get a generic list read off a page.

Step 4: Follow-ups on what you actually say

This is where practice gets real. The interviewer digs into your answers. A vague story gets a "walk me through your part in that." If you say "we fixed it," expect to be asked what you did first. Follow-ups are hard to rehearse alone, and they are where many answers fall apart.

Step 5: Questions about the gaps

Where the job asks for something your resume does not show yet, the interviewer handles it the way a real hiring manager would. It digs into related experience in the same field instead of just asking "do you have a degree," and it asks plainly about any license the job needs. Hearing that question on a practice call is far better than hearing it cold.

How long the interview runs

Step 6, on every plan: the live AI coach on the same call

On every plan, the AI coach comes on the same call right after the interviewer hangs up. No app to open, no link to click. Your coach works from your resume, the job posting and what you actually said.

  • It does not open with a grade. It asks how you think it went and gives you room to spot things yourself.
  • It shows where an answer missed what the job is asking for.
  • It points you to a stronger story from your own resume when you had a better one to tell.
  • When a gap comes up, like a degree the job prefers, it shows how to speak to it honestly.
  • It rewrites your answer using a story you told. If a number is missing, it leaves a blank for you to fill in instead of guessing.

Step 7, on every plan: your written report and full transcript

After every call, on every plan, a written report lands in your email with the full transcript, so you can see exactly what you said. Read it on the bus, before the real interview, or right before you call again. The example report on our Interview Coach page shows the layout: a short headline, what you did well and what to work on next.

After that: call again

You can call back and practice again within your monthly minutes: 90 a month on Starter, 240 on Standard or 480 on Pro. Interview Coach plans and their pricing are coming soon. If you apply for a different job or update your resume, you can rebuild your interview. Your setup page shows how many rebuilds you have left.

Questions

Can I retry a hard question to get a better answer?

Yes, in a few ways. On every plan, the coach can have you try an answer again on the same call, using a story from your own resume. On any plan, you can call back and practice again within your monthly minutes. You can also rebuild your interview with a new job posting or an updated resume, and your setup page shows how many rebuilds you have left.

Is the interviewer a real person?

No. The interviewer and the coach are AI, built to feel like the real interview so the practice carries over.

Do I need an app or a webcam?

No. You call one phone number from your own phone and say your six-digit access code. No app, no webcam, no login on the call.

Do I get the coach and a report on every plan?

Yes. Every plan includes the live AI coach on the same call, and a written report after every call with the full transcript.
