How it works

Your people practice a real conversation on a real phone call.

Every person on your team gets a phone number and a code. They dial in and talk to an AI buyer built from your world, in an AI voice that sounds like a real person. Then they get feedback on how it went. There is no app to install and no login for your team. It works for any job where the conversation is the work: sales, service, care and more.

    What you can count on

    Built to coach your people, not to rank them

    Feedback on the call, not a ranking

    Reports show what went well, what to work on next, and a checklist of moments: done, missed, did not come up, or not checked. People are not ranked against each other.

    Checked against the call

    Coaching in the written report is checked against the transcript of what was actually said. If a question is not something a call can show, the report says so and leaves it out of the count.

    Your team can tell us

    Each line on a report can be marked Right or Not right from a link in the report. We review what comes in, a mark changes only after a person approves it, and the manager can see what was sent.

    Recorded, and the line says so

    Practice calls are recorded, and the line tells you before the call starts. How long we keep recordings is in our Privacy Notice.

    Your playbook stays yours

    Bring your own playbook and your reports reflect it. You can edit your own material any time.

    Nothing to install

    A phone and a code is all your team needs. Practice in the truck, in the parking lot, or at a desk.
