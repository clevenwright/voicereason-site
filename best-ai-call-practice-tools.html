1. VoiceReason

Your team dials one number and practices with an AI voice that sounds like a real person. No app or laptop needed to practice, just a phone. After every call, a written report goes out by email, and on Practice + Coach, a coach comes on the same call right after to talk it through. Plans are $69 and $129 per seat per month, month to month.

Best for: teams on the road or away from a desk, and any job where the conversation is the work. Job seekers: see Interview Coach.

2. Cold Call Gym

Also practice by phone: call a number from any phone, with no signup for the first call, and get a text message report afterward. Their site also offers real-time coaching, and full reports and transcripts when you sign in. Team Pro is $99 per seat per month with a 3 seat minimum.

Best for: cold calling practice by phone, with a free start for individuals and team plans.

3. Hyperbound

Practice happens online, with a free plan of prebuilt roleplays and scorecards. Paid plans add custom scorecards and CRM features. Team pricing is not published; they ask you to book a demo.

Best for: sales teams that want practice tied to their CRM and to scoring of real calls.

4. Second Nature

A browser-based practice tool with scores and written reports. We did not find published prices; their site offers a demo.

Best for: large companies with teams spread across many countries.

5. Yoodli

Practice in a browser, with written feedback after each session. Individuals can start free, and individual plans are published.

Best for: revenue and training teams that practice inside Zoom, Teams or Slack, and individuals who want a free start for interviews or pitches.

6. PitchMonster

Practice online, with scorecards built on frameworks like MEDDIC, SPIN or Sandler, and transcripts. AI coach conversations come with the higher plan. Pricing is a quote by plan, seats and contract length.

Best for: sales teams that want scorecards built on frameworks like MEDDIC, SPIN or Sandler.

7. Quantified

Practice online with AI personas built for your market, scored against your approved criteria. Pricing is quoted per organization.

Best for: life sciences and other regulated teams.

When another tool may fit better

  • You want practice tied to your CRM and your recorded sales calls: look at Hyperbound or PitchMonster.
  • You want cold calling practice by phone with a free start: look at Cold Call Gym.
  • You work in life sciences or another regulated field: look at Quantified.
