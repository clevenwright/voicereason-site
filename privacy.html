This notice explains what information VoiceReason.AI collects, why we use it, who receives it, how long we keep it, and the choices available to you. It covers the VoiceReason website, accounts, practice calls placed to VoiceReason phone numbers, the public demo line, the waitlist, and text messages we send with your consent.

Who we are

VoiceReason.AI is a product of Pyrigen Incorporated, a Texas corporation. When this notice says "Pyrigen," "VoiceReason," "we," or "us," it means Pyrigen Incorporated as the operator of VoiceReason.AI.

VoiceReason is currently offered for use in the United States. We do not intentionally offer the account service to users outside the United States unless we expressly make it available in that location. Account signup may require a United States residence or business location, United States billing information, and a representation that the service is being purchased for use in the United States.

What we collect

  • Contact and profile information. Your name, email address, and, if you choose to provide them, your phone number, company, role, and information about the kinds of buyers or accounts you work with.
  • Account and subscription information. Login and account identifiers, subscription tier, billing status, renewal and cancellation status, plan-change history, and records showing the subscription terms and consent presented to you.
  • Payment information. Full payment-card details are handled by our payment processor rather than stored as VoiceReason account data. We may receive limited transaction information from the payment processor, such as payment status, transaction identifiers, billing country, and limited payment-method details needed to manage billing and support.
  • Buyer personas and practice inputs. The personas you create and whatever information you choose to put into them, including details about your industry, accounts, prospects, or deals.
  • Practice-call information. Call audio, transcripts, call timing and duration, session-use records, session-restoration records, and the coaching reports generated from your calls.
  • Text-message information. Your phone number, whether and when you opted in or out, delivery and suppression status, and records needed to honor messaging preferences.
  • Waitlist and communications. Information you provide when joining a waitlist, contacting support, or otherwise communicating with us.
  • Technical and security information. Server and product logs, IP address, device or browser information, authentication events, provider call identifiers, error codes, and similar information needed to operate, troubleshoot, secure, and prevent abuse of the service.
  • Website analytics. Aggregate, privacy-preserving measurements of how the VoiceReason website is used, such as pages viewed, referring source, approximate region, and general device type. Our website analytics provider does not set cookies and does not create a persistent identifier that follows you across websites or across visits. We use this information to understand which pages are useful and to improve the site.

Calls, recordings, and transcripts

Practice calls placed to VoiceReason numbers are recorded and transcribed. Recording and transcription are necessary to run the practice conversation and produce the coaching report; there is no unrecorded version of the practice-call service.

At the start of each call, we tell you: "Thanks for calling VoiceReason. This call is recorded and transcribed for sales coaching. By continuing, you consent." If you do not consent, do not continue the call.

The buyer you speak with is an artificial voice generated by software, not a human being.

We keep call audio and transcripts in our own systems for 12 months and then delete them, subject to legal requirements and limited technical processes such as backups, security logs, or deletion queues that may take additional time to cycle out. A copy also sits with the voice provider that carries and transcribes the call, and that provider retains its copy under its own terms rather than ours, as described below. Coaching reports and non-content session records are retained as described below.

A practice call may feel private, but anything you say can be recorded and transcribed. Sales rehearsals can easily include real prospect names, customer information, pricing, employer information, or other confidential details. Do not say or enter information that you are not authorized to disclose to VoiceReason and its service providers for this purpose.

We do not publish your practice calls, transcripts, coaching reports, or personas as public examples or marketing material without your permission.

How AI and service providers process call information

VoiceReason depends on third-party technology to carry calls, generate or synthesize voice, transcribe speech, operate the artificial buyer, create coaching output, host data, and secure the service. As a result, call audio, transcripts, personas, and related information may be processed by the service providers that perform those functions for us.

We disclose information to those providers only as reasonably necessary for the services they provide to VoiceReason and subject to the contracts, settings, and legal requirements that apply to those relationships.

VoiceReason does not use customer call audio or transcripts to train publicly available general-purpose AI models, and we do not authorize our service providers to use customer call audio or transcripts for that purpose. If a provider offers controls that restrict model training or secondary use, we intend to configure those controls consistently with this commitment.

We may use aggregated, de-identified, or non-content operational information to understand and improve the product.

This notice does not promise that ordinary audio recording is a biometric process. If VoiceReason or a provider creates a voiceprint, speaker-identification profile, speaker embedding used to identify a person, or other biometric identifier from a caller's voice, we will address any additional notice, consent, use, disclosure, security, and retention requirements that apply before using that functionality for VoiceReason.

Text messages

If you separately opt in, we send you a text message containing a link to your coaching report after a practice call. Message frequency varies and is expected to be about one message per completed practice call. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. You can also turn text messages off in your account or communicate a clear opt-out request through a reasonable support channel.

Text messages are optional. Consent to receive them is not a condition of purchase, and the core VoiceReason service can be used without report-link text messages.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors who provide support services, such as our messaging provider, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

The public demo line

Our public demo number is open to anyone and does not require an account. Demo calls are recorded and transcribed on the same basis described above, and we provide the recording and transcription notice at the start of the call. We do not send text messages to demo callers based only on calling the demo line.

Why we use information

  • To provide the service: create or load buyer personas, connect and run practice calls, transcribe calls, produce coaching reports, track session use, restore sessions affected by detected system or provider failures, and provide account features.
  • To manage subscriptions: process payments through our payment provider, maintain billing and consent records, handle renewals, cancellations, plan changes, and customer support.
  • To communicate with you: send account and service notices, respond to support requests, contact waitlist users about access, and send report-link text messages when you have opted in.
  • To secure and operate VoiceReason: authenticate users, troubleshoot failures, prevent abuse, investigate security issues, enforce our Terms, and maintain the reliability of the service.
  • To improve VoiceReason: evaluate how the product performs and improve features using aggregated, de-identified, non-content operational, or other information as described in this notice.
  • To comply with law and protect rights: maintain records we are legally required to keep, respond to lawful requests, and protect users, Pyrigen, and others.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information for money.
  • We do not share your information with third parties for their own marketing or promotional use.
  • We do not build advertising profiles about you, and we do not use cookies or cross-site tracking technologies.
  • We do not publish your calls, transcripts, coaching reports, or personas as public content without your permission.
  • We do not use customer call audio or transcripts to train publicly available general-purpose AI models.
  • We do not treat text-message consent as permission to market unrelated products to you.

Who we share information with

We use service providers to operate VoiceReason. Depending on the feature, those providers may include:

  • payment processors for subscription billing;
  • telephony, synthetic-voice, speech, and transcription providers for calls;
  • AI or software providers used to operate the artificial buyer or generate coaching output;
  • messaging and email providers;
  • cloud hosting, database, storage, monitoring, and security providers; and
  • professional advisers and support providers where reasonably necessary for legal, accounting, security, or operational purposes.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, or government requests; investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents; protect the rights or safety of users, Pyrigen, or others; or complete a corporate transaction such as a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets, subject to applicable law.

How long we keep information

  • Call audio and transcripts: we delete these from VoiceReason's own systems 12 months after the call. Our voice provider keeps its own copy under its own terms — see Providers that process call audio, below.
  • Coaching reports: while your account remains open, unless you delete them earlier where that feature is available or a longer period is required for a legal reason.
  • Buyer personas: while your account remains open, unless you delete them earlier.
  • Session and usage records: generally 24 months, including non-content records such as session ID, account ID, plan version, provider call ID, timestamps, duration, termination reason, whether the session was counted, and any restoration or credit. We keep these records to administer session balances, investigate product failures, respond to disputes, and maintain accurate billing and usage records.
  • Account and billing records: while your account is open and afterward for as long as reasonably necessary for tax, accounting, fraud prevention, disputes, and legal obligations.
  • Automatic-renewal consent records: at least three years, or one year after the subscription contract ends, whichever period is longer, and longer if another applicable law requires it.
  • Text-message consent and opt-out records: three years under our current retention policy, unless a longer period is reasonably necessary or required for legal compliance, dispute defense, or to continue honoring an opt-out.
  • Waitlist information: until you ask us to remove it or it is clearly no longer needed for the purpose for which it was collected.

Providers that process call audio. Our voice provider receives call audio and transcripts in order to carry and transcribe the call, and retains what it receives under its own terms rather than ours. On our current plan with that provider, it retains those inputs indefinitely rather than on a fixed deletion schedule. We do not authorize it to use customer call audio or transcripts to train its models, and we have disabled the model-training control it makes available to us.

Deletion from active systems does not always remove information immediately from disaster-recovery backups or logs. Information in those systems is isolated from ordinary use and ages out under the applicable backup or security cycle.

Your choices and requests

  • Turn text messages off at any time by using account settings, replying STOP, or making a clear opt-out request through a reasonable support channel.
  • Delete a buyer persona through the product when that control is available.
  • Cancel automatic renewal online from your account. Cancellation stops future renewal rather than deleting your account immediately.
  • Ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you by contacting us. We may need to verify your identity, and some information may be retained where applicable law permits or requires it.
  • Ask us to remove you from the waitlist.

Depending on where you live and whether a particular privacy law applies to Pyrigen, you may have additional privacy rights. We will honor rights that apply to VoiceReason and your information. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right provided by applicable law.
