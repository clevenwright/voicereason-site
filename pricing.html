What a seat is

One seat is one person who makes practice calls, with one access code. Your own manager login does not use a seat. If you have eight people practicing, you need eight seats.

What every plan includes

  • Practice on a real phone call. Your people dial one number and say their access code. No app, no download, no login for them.
  • A buyer you build by describing the customer your team really faces, or one of ours to start.
  • Your own sales or service playbook, if you have one, as the checklist every report is measured against.
  • A manager view with every team member's calls in one place.
  • Calls are recorded, and the line says so. Your calls are not used to train models for anyone else.

Questions

How much does VoiceReason cost?

Practice is $69 per seat per month, or $759 per seat per year. Practice + Coach is $129 per seat per month.

Do minutes belong to each person?

No. Every seat on your plan adds its minutes to one team pool, so someone who needs extra practice can use more. Minutes reset each month and do not roll over.

Does the coach use up practice minutes?

Yes. Coaching time uses the same minutes as the practice call and counts toward the 60 minute call limit on Practice + Coach.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time. Canceling stops the next charge, and your plan stays active through the period you already paid for.

Do I need to talk to sales to buy?

No. You create your account, choose your plan and seats, and pay by card. Questions? Call (945) 26-COACH.
