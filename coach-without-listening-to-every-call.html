Guide · Coaching from reports

How can I coach my team without listening to every call?

Coach from written reports instead of from hours of recordings. Have your team practice the calls that matter most by phone, and let each practice call end with a written report by email. You read the reports, spot the pattern, and talk with one person about one call. You stay close to how your team sounds without sitting through every conversation.

Why listening to every call does not work

Listening to calls is useful. It is also slow. An hour of call reviews covers only a few calls, and the moments you care about, like the price question or the upset customer, are buried in the middle.

And by the time you hear a real call, the customer is already gone.

Coach on practice calls, not just real ones

Practice calls flip that. Your team member runs the hard part of the call on purpose, before a real customer is on the line. Every practice call ends with a written report, so you read instead of listening start to finish.

Here is what to look for when you read reports:

  • Who is practicing. Your Calls page shows who is practicing and which calls they ran. If someone has not practiced, that is your first conversation.
  • Which calls they pick. If a team member keeps avoiding the price call, that tells you where they feel weak.
  • How each one went. Read the report. Look for the same note showing up across several calls or several people.
  • Whether it matches how you sell. If you added your playbook, every report measures the call against the way your team sells.

A 20-minute weekly coaching routine

  1. Open your Calls page. Scan who practiced this week and which calls they ran.
  2. Read three reports. Pick one strong call, one weak call, and one from your newest team member.
  3. Find one pattern. Is the team rushing past questions? Talking price too early? Pick one thing.
  4. Have one short talk. Share the pattern with the team, or sit down with one person and go through one report together.
  5. Set next week's practice. Ask everyone to run that same call again until it feels natural.

You are still the coach. You are just coaching from a summary instead of a recording.

Keep some real-call review, just less of it

You do not have to stop listening to real calls. Use them to check that practice is carrying over. Pick one real call a week from the person you are most focused on. Compare it with their practice reports. If the real call sounds like the practice call, that is a good sign. If it does not, pick a different call for them to practice next week.
