What can new hires practice on their own before they talk to real customers?

The calls that make new people nervous are the ones they have not had yet: the first discovery call, the customer who asks about price in the first minute, the one who says "just send me something." With VoiceReason, a new team member practices those calls on their own, out loud, on a real phone call, before a real customer ever hears them.

The calls worth practicing first

  • The opening. Who they are, why they are calling, and getting to a real question before the customer tunes out.
  • Discovery. Asking what the customer actually needs, and following up on the answer instead of jumping to a pitch.
  • The early price question. Answering it without rushing, and getting back to what the customer needs.
  • The brush-off. "Just email me something" or "we are all set," and keeping the conversation going without pushing.
  • The next step. Asking for the meeting, the visit or the decision, plainly.
  • The upset customer. For service teams: calming the call down, owning the problem, and offering a way forward.

Why on their own, and why by phone

Managers rarely have time to role-play every call with every new person. And a new hire who practices only with a manager tends to practice the calls that are comfortable to rehearse in front of the boss. Practicing alone takes that pressure off, so they try the call again until it feels natural.

Most of the job happens on the phone or face to face, so the practice should sound like it. Your team member calls one number from the phone already in their pocket. They can practice from a desk, from the truck between appointments, or from home the night before.

How it works

  1. Describe the customer. In a sentence or a page, say who your team talks to and what they push back on. Or start from one of ours.
  2. Add your playbook if you have one. Every report then measures the call against the way your team sells.
  3. Your team member dials one number from their own phone and says their six-digit access code. No app, no download, no login.
  4. They talk to the customer. An AI voice that sounds like a real person stays in character and pushes back the way real customers do.
  5. They get a written report by email after every call. On Practice + Coach, a coach also comes on the same call right after the practice call ends and talks it through with them.

What the manager sees

Every practice call and its report are in one place, so you can see who is practicing, which calls they ran, and how each one went. New people can run the same tough call ten times in a week instead of waiting for a live one.
